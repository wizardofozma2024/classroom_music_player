THREE LOOP OPTIONS!

Click the loop button (🔁) to cycle through:

1. **Loop Off** (🔁 gray)
   - Playlist plays once and stops

2. **Loop Playlist** (🔁 green)
   - Entire playlist repeats forever
   - Perfect for continuous background music

3. **Loop One Song** (🔂 green)
   - Current song repeats forever
   - Great for learning lyrics or just loving one song!
